## Brand & Style

This design system translates frontier quantum research and deep-tech instrumentation into a pristine, high-luminance cleanroom environment. The design movement marries **Scientific Minimalism** with **Technical Precision**, projecting an atmosphere of rigorous experimentation, institutional clarity, and cutting-edge computational capability. 

The aesthetic strips away dark-mode sci-fi tropes in favor of an exacting, peer-reviewed laboratory ethos: pure white cleanrooms, precision optics, laser diagnostics, and structured analytical consoles. Surfaces are cool, immaculate, and surgical. Interactions evoke high-frequency data capture, tactile precision switches, and calibrated telemetry. The target audience encompasses quantum physicists, computational researchers, hardware engineers, and enterprise R&D partners who demand dense data legibility, low cognitive fatigue over extended analysis sessions, and immediate structural hierarchy.

## Colors

The chromatic architecture is anchored by an electric photon spectrum cast against hyper-clean optical white substrates.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Accent (`#0284c7`)**: Coherent blue laser core. Drives primary interactive states, key focal points, confirmed actions, and active navigation indicators.
- **Secondary Accent (`#38bdf8`)**: Ionized cyan beam. Utilized for high-energy telemetry markers, focus rings, state glow indicators, and live data stream highlights.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#0369a1`)**: Deep collimated sapphire. Provides high-contrast interactive states, hover triggers, active data tabs, and condensed technical micro-accents.
- **Neutral Primary (`#0f172a`)**: Deep vacuum slate. High-density primary text, terminal structural headers, and sharp coordinate grids.

### Surface Tiers & Neutral Scales
- **Surface Canvas (`#ffffff`)**: The baseline cleanroom foundation. Zero warmth, pure optical white.
- **Surface Level 1 / Sub-surface (`#f8fafc`)**: Analytical containers, side rails, peripheral inspection trays.
- **Surface Level 2 / Card Surface (`#f1f5f9`)**: Layered analytical modules, data matrices, parameter input bays.
- **Surface Level 3 / Active Surface (`#e2e8f0`)**: Inactive input fields, table header bands, structural card dividers.
- **Border Structural (`#e2e8f0`)**: Calibrated 1px boundary rules separating telemetry modules.
- **Border High-Precision (`#cbd5e1`)**: Hover rules and structural component boundaries requiring sharp delineation.

### Text Contrast Roles
- **Text Primary (`#0f172a`)**: Maximum legibility for values, titles, and imperative labels.
- **Text Secondary (`#334155`)**: Sub-headers, technical descriptors, unit dimensions.
- **Text Tertiary / Muted (`#64748b`)**: Inactive telemetry, baseline grid coordinates, timestamp records.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy implements three distinct operational tiers to maintain rigorous separation between institutional presence, operational reading, and empirical instrumentation:

1. **Display & Structural Titles (`Space Grotesk`)**: Geometric, sharp, and forward-looking. Provides technological authority across dashboard banners, experiment IDs, and metric section breaks.
2. **Body Prose & System Documentation (`Inter`)**: Engineered for neutral, high-density legibility. Neutralizes glyph fatigue in dense experimental abstracts, configuration documentation, and logs.
3. **Telemetry, Controls & Micro-Metrics (`JetBrains Mono`)**: Monospaced precision. All mathematical notation, state outputs, matrix indices, coordinate readouts, and status chips strictly mandate this font family with uppercase styling and tabular lining numbers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors an optical breadboard: disciplined, modular, and structurally aligned to a dense 4px/8px coordinate matrix.

### Grid Dynamics
- **Desktop (>= 1280px)**: 12-column fluid grid. Gutter: `2rem` (`space-xl` scaled / `gutter-lg`), margin: `3rem` (`margin-lg`). Outer bounds anchor at 1600px maximum width for precision visualization consoles, with optional full-width expansion for multi-stream quantum state matrices.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8-column layout. Gutter: `1.5rem` (`gutter-md`), margin: `2rem` (`margin-md`). Sidebar navigation collapses to a high-density icon bar.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout. Gutter: `1rem` (`gutter`), margin: `1rem` (`margin`). Multi-column telemetry grids stack into singular modular breadboards.

### Rhythm Principles
- Component internal padding strictly uses `space-xs` (4px) to `space-md` (16px) to maintain instrumentation density.
- Analytical cards employ `space-lg` (24px) for containment boundaries.
- Section boundaries and module clusters preserve `space-xl` (40px) separation to prevent operational clutter.

## Elevation & Depth

In a cleanroom laboratory environment, elevation is conveyed through **chromatic boundary rings, low-contrast structural outlines, and crisp optical back-diffusions**, entirely avoiding heavy muddy drop shadows.

### Depth Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Basebed)**: Flat `#ffffff` optical canvas. Ground plane for background grids, axis markings, and coordinate rulers.
- **Level 1 (Docked Containers)**: Surface `#f8fafc` bounded by a 1px continuous hairline outline of `#e2e8f0`. Zero shadow. Used for persistent consoles, side panels, and utility drawers.
- **Level 2 (Active Cards & Instrumentation Panels)**: Surface `#ffffff` elevated above `#f8fafc` containers with a 1px border of `#cbd5e1` and an ambient cleanroom rim-shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Level 3 (Interactive Hover & Flyout Diagnostics)**: Surface `#ffffff` backed by an active cyan-tinted optical dispersion: `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.04)`, bounded by `#0284c7` border-accented rings.
- **Level 4 (Modals & Calibration Overlays)**: Surface `#ffffff` with a semi-opaque cleanroom light backdrop (`rgba(248, 250, 252, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`). Shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.

## Shapes

The design system implements a strict **Soft Precision (`1`)** geometry. 

- Baseline UI elements (buttons, text inputs, telemetry pills, matrix toggles) feature `0.25rem` (4px) corner radii, communicating machined, calibrated industrial instrumentation.
- Structural cards, analytical modals, and container bays scale to `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / 8px).
- Complex canvas viewports and high-level frame containers use `rounded-xl` (`0.75rem` / 12px).
- Full circular/pill radius is exclusively reserved for status indicator pings and dynamic wave amplitude tags.

## Components

### Buttons & Trigger Elements
- **Primary Action**: Background `#0284c7`, text `#ffffff`, border 1px solid `#0369a1`. Hover transitions to background `#0369a1` with an outer optical aura: `box-shadow: 0 0 12px rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.35)`. Active: `#075985`.
- **Secondary (Cleanroom Standard)**: Background `#ffffff`, text `#0f172a`, border 1px solid `#cbd5e1`. Hover shifts background to `#f8fafc`, border to `#0284c7`, text to `#0284c7`.
- **Ghost / Analytical**: Monospaced label (`JetBrains Mono`), text `#334155`, transparent background. Hover adds `#f1f5f9` fill and `#0f172a` text color.

### Chips & Status Indicators
- Structure: 4px radius (`rounded`), padding `2px 8px`, `label-sm` monospaced type.
- **Quantum Nominal / Active**: Background `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.12)`, text `#0369a1`, border 1px solid `rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.3)`. Emits a live cyan status dot (`#38bdf8`) with an animated 1.5s pulse.
- **Calibrating / Warning**: Background `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.10)`, text `#b45309`, border 1px solid `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.3)`.
- **Critical / Decoherence**: Background `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.10)`, text `#b91c1c`, border 1px solid `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.3)`.

### Cards & Analytical Bays
- Pure `#ffffff` surface, 8px radius (`rounded-lg`), 1px solid `#e2e8f0` border.
- Cards feature an optional hairline header rail: top padding 12px, bottom padding 12px, border-bottom 1px solid `#f1f5f9` with monospaced uppercase section titles (`label-md`) in `#64748b`.
- Interactive cards receive a `#38bdf8` top-border highlight (2px height) on hover with a faint ambient glow.

### Input Fields & Parameter Controls
- Background `#f8fafc`, text `#0f172a`, placeholder `#94a3b8`, border 1px solid `#cbd5e1`, 4px radius (`rounded`).
- Active focus state: Surface becomes `#ffffff`, border switches to `#0284c7`, accompanied by an external precision ring: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 2px rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.25)`.
- Numerical input controls use `JetBrains Mono` with embedded scientific unit suffixes fixed right (`#64748b`).

### Checkboxes, Radios & Switches
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Unchecked state uses `#ffffff` fill with 1.5px `#cbd5e1` boundary. Checked state fills `#0284c7` with white check/dot icon and an ambient outer glow `0 0 8px rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.4)`.
- **Toggle Switches**: Track uses `#e2e8f0` (unchecked) and switches to `#0284c7` (checked). The thumb is pure `#ffffff` with a cleanroom tactile drop `0 1px 2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.2)`.

### Tables & Data Streams
- Row striping: alternating `#ffffff` and `#f8fafc`.
- Table header: `#f1f5f9`, typography `label-sm` in `#475569`, border-bottom 1px solid `#cbd5e1`.
- Row hover: `#f0f9ff` (pale optical sky) with a 2px vertical indicator bar in `#0284c7` along the left cell boundary.
